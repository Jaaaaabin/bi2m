## Dimensional standards

### Office / workstation area

- Neufert states that, under newer workplace regulations, **there are no longer fixed minimum workplace dimensions**, but sufficient working and movement areas are required, especially for computer-screen workstations and according to DIN EN / workplace rules. page_0247  
- A computer workstation must include work area, shelf/furniture area, furniture function area, movement area, and traffic/passages. page_0247  
- At the workstation, provide a **free movement area of at least 1.5 m²**. page_0248  
- Desktop working area: **at least 120 × 80 cm** for predominantly screen work with few documents; for specialist employees, **at least 200 × 80 cm**. page_0248  
- Desk/chair ergonomics: variable desk **60–78 cm**, variable chair **42–54 cm**; fixed desk option **72 cm**, variable chair **42–50 cm**, footstool **0–15 cm**. page_0248  

### Ceiling height

- For office buildings, Neufert gives **2.75 m ceiling height** according to Vst regulation, allowing later raised floors and deeper suspended ceilings. page_0255  
- For predominantly sedentary office activities, reduction by **25 cm** is possible, but the clear height must be **at least 2.50 m**. page_0255  
- Corridors and sanitary facilities may be **2.30 m high** where used for installation runs. page_0255  

### Modular / planning grids

- Office buildings are designed for flexibility and reversibility so that user units can change without major works. page_0246  
- Laboratory planning grids use a **120 × 120 cm** planning module; good structural grids for lab buildings include **7.20 × 7.20 m**, **7.20 × 8.40 m**, and **8.40 × 8.40 m**. page_0218  
- For this office adjoining laboratories, coordinate partition/service zones with the lab module where penetrations or lab services occur. page_0218

## W1 — interior partition shared with **Flur EG West / Hallway Ground floor West**, with 1 basic_door

- **Construction type:** Non-load-bearing separating wall / stud partition or massive partition suitable for office-to-circulation separation. Neufert permits non-load-bearing separating walls as stud partitions of different qualities. page_0526  
- **Fire class:** In a 5-storey building likely falling under higher building-class requirements, separating walls between commercial units or rooms used otherwise must correspond to the quality of the load-bearing components of the storey; openings must be limited to the required number/size and closed by at least fire-retarding, sealed, self-closing components. page_0526  
- **Recommended fire performance for specification:** use **F 90-A / EI 90** where the wall is part of a required separation in a 5-storey office building, because Neufert states load-bearing/bracing walls in building class 5 must be fireproof and fire-resistance classes include F 90 / R 90 / EI 90. page_0526, page_0524

- **Acoustic detailing:** doors and windows have much lower sound insulation than walls and strongly reduce the resulting partition performance; therefore the door on W1 must be the acoustic weak-point priority. page_0490  
- **Build-up / thickness:** Neufert does not give one single required thickness for an office-hallway partition; it shows that non-load-bearing separating walls may be constructed as stud partitions of various qualities and that fire/sound performance must be verified by classified systems. page_0526

## W4 — exterior wall / façade with 2 basic_window → exterior

- **Construction type:** Exterior wall may be single-leaf with thermal insulation/weather protection, single-leaf with external thermal insulation, or cavity wall; Neufert describes single-leaf external walls with additional insulation and cavity walls with inner load-bearing wall, outer weather-protection leaf, air gap, and ties. page_0087  
- **Cavity-wall rules if used:** outer facing leaf minimum **90 mm**; air gap **minimum 40 mm**, maximum **150 mm**; provide non-rusting ties, vertical expansion joints, and ventilation/drainage openings at top and bottom. page_0087  
- **Fire class / façade fire:** For buildings beyond classes 1–3, non-load-bearing external walls and non-load-bearing parts of load-bearing external walls must be made of non-combustible, at least fire-retarding materials; external wall claddings, insulation, and support construction must be flame-resistant. page_0526  
- **Airborne sound vs exterior noise:** Neufert states external noise protection is achieved by building layout, sound-insulated external walls, especially sound-insulated doors/windows, façade-side sound-insulating blinds, and landscaping/noise barriers. page_0492  
- **Window sound insulation classes:** Window sound insulation classes range from class 0 **20–24 dB** to class 6 **50 dB**; noise level range IV requires window Rw **40 dB** in occupied rooms of dwellings, and range V requires **45 dB**. Use project outdoor-noise survey to select the class. page_0112  
- **Opaque wall U-value:** Neufert gives the U-value calculation method, \( U = 1 / (R_{si} + R + R_{se}) \), and states EnEV verification is required, but the retrieved Neufert text does **not** provide a single office-façade opaque-wall U-limit to prescribe here. page_0483, page_0486  
- **Thermal-bridge note:** Thermal bridges lower inner surface temperature and can cause condensation and mould; external corners are typical thermal bridges, and connections must be thermally detailed. page_0485, page_0486  
- **Airtightness:** New buildings must have permanently airtight heat-transmitting envelope joints; external windows and roof windows must meet EnEV airtightness requirements, and airtightness testing can be used in energy verification. page_0486  
- **Sun-shading approach:** Neufert shows awnings, external louvred blinds, markisolette blinds, and impact panes shielding external sun protection. External shading is preferred over internal glare-only blinds because internal venetian blinds allow heat build-up behind the window. page_0109, page_0111  
- **Moisture/vapour:** Condensation in external elements should be avoided; vapour barriers must be on the warm side where required, and layer sequence must avoid condensation in the element. page_0484

## Window 1 on W4

Because there are **2 windows on W4**, the daylight/window-area requirement is divided between them.

- **Minimum window area by occupied-room rule:** State building regulations for occupied rooms require structural window apertures of **1/8 or 1/10 of room plan area**. For this 11.44 m² office, **1/8 = 1.43 m² total**, or **0.715 m² per window** if divided equally between two windows. page_0109  
- **Workroom daylight-area guidance:** For room depths up to **8 m**, Neufert estimates window areas of approximately **16–20% of plan area**; for 11.44 m², this is **1.83–2.29 m² total**, or **0.92–1.14 m² per window**. page_0508  
- **Minimum glass height:** For workrooms with dimensions similar to residential rooms, the minimum height of the glass area is **1.3 m**. page_0109  
- **Derived minimum per-window glass size:** If using the Neufert **1.3 m** glass-height rule, each window should provide at least about **0.70–0.88 m width × 1.30 m height** to meet the stricter **16–20% workroom daylight guidance** per window. page_0109, page_0508  
- **65% room-width rule:** The total window width before finishes should be **≥ 65% of the room width**; since W4 room width is not supplied, final width must be checked once the W4 length/room width is known. page_0507  
- **Sill height:** Visual connection should be at eye level; workroom sill heights **0.85–1.25 m** are recommended. page_0109  
- **Reference daylight plane:** DIN 5034 daylight evaluation assumes a windowsill/reference level of **0.85 m**. page_0507  
- **Glazing type / U-value:** External windows in heated rooms must have at least insulating or double glazing; EnEV 2009 maximum U-value for external windows/French windows in heated non-residential zones above 19°C is **Uw ≤ 1.30 W/m²K**, and glazing **Ug ≤ 1.10 W/m²K**. page_0111  
- **Sound glazing:** Select the window sound-insulation class by outdoor-noise level; class 3 gives **35–39 dB**, class 4 **40–44 dB**, class 5 **45–49 dB**, class 6 **50 dB**. page_0112  
- **Sun shading:** Use external louvred blind, awning, markisolette, or impact-pane-protected external blind; internal venetian blind provides mainly glare protection and permits heat build-up. page_0109, page_0111  
- **Escape-window rule:** If this window is intended as an escape opening, it must have a clear opening of at least **0.9 × 1.2 m** and sill height maximum **1.2 m** above floor level. page_0109

## Acoustics

- DIN 4109 values in Neufert cover neighbouring residential and working areas using weighted building sound reduction **R’w**, including secondary transmission. page_0490
