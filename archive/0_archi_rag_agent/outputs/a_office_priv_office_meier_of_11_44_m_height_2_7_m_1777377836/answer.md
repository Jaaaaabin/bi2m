### W4 — exterior wall / facade with 2 basic_window to exterior

- **Construction type:** insulated external wall / façade with integrated window openings. Neufert notes that the façade grid and fitting-out grid should align so partitions can connect to façade profiles, and that sound-insulated façade profiles are preferable where partitions meet the façade. page_0252  
- **Thermal insulation / U-value:** for external walls in residential buildings and zones of other buildings with indoor temperatures **>19°C**, Neufert’s EnEV 2009 refurbishment/replacement table gives **Umax 0.24 W/m²K**; for non-residential zones at **12–19°C**, **Umax 0.35 W/m²K** is listed. For an occupied heated office, use **≤0.24 W/m²K** as the envelope target unless whole-building EnEV verification gives another value. page_0091  
- **New-building energy verification:** EnEV for non-residential buildings uses whole-building primary-energy and envelope verification; U-values for individual components are inputs to this verification. page_0486  
- **Thermal bridges:** detail window reveals, floor edges, and wall junctions to avoid additional heat loss, low internal surface temperature, condensation, and mould; thermal bridges may be considered by increasing U-values or by separate verification. page_0486  
- **Fire class / combustibility:** for buildings except classes 1–3, non-load-bearing external walls and non-load-bearing parts of load-bearing external walls are to be of non-combustible, at least fire-retarding materials; external wall claddings, insulation, and support construction must be flame-resistant. page_0526  
- **Airborne sound / outdoor noise:** Neufert states that protection from external noise is achieved by occupied rooms away from noise, sound-insulated external walls, sound-insulated doors/windows, façade-side sound-insulating blinds, and landscaping/noise barriers. page_0492  
- **Window acoustic class:** if outdoor noise is unknown, Neufert’s VDI 2719 table gives window sound-insulation classes from **25–29 dB** for class 1 to **50 dB** for class 6; select after the façade noise level is known. page_0112  
- **Sun shading:** external louvred blinds and awnings are shown as suitable sun-protection measures; Neufert notes that internal blinds mainly provide glare protection because heat builds up behind the glass. page_0109  
- **Window placement:** avoid staggered window/insulation planes because Neufert warns that this leads to expensive and defect-prone insulation and weatherproofing construction. page_0111

### Window 1 on W4

- **Sill height:** specify sill height within **0.85–1.25 m**; DIN 5034 daylight reference plane assumes **0.85 m**. page_0109, page_0507  
- **Glass height:** for workrooms with dimensions corresponding to residential rooms, Neufert gives minimum glass-area height **1.3 m**. page_0109  
- **Width formula from Neufert data:** using the 1/8 rule and 1.3 m glass height, Window 1 needs about **0.72 / 1.3 = 0.55 m** clear glass width; using the 1/10 rule, about **0.57 / 1.3 = 0.44 m** clear glass width. page_0109

- **Thermal glazing:** external windows of heated rooms must use at least insulating or double glazing; EnEV 2009 table gives **Umax 1.30 W/m²K** for external windows/French windows in heated zones **>19°C**, and **Ug 1.10 W/m²K** for glazing. page_0111  
- **Spacer / condensation:** use thermally improved spacers to reduce condensation at the glass-frame transition. page_0118  
- **Solar control:** use external louvred blind, awning, or markisolette; internal venetian blinds are mainly glare protection because heat builds up behind the window. page_0109  
- **Acoustic glazing:** if exterior noise is significant, select a tested acoustic glazing; Neufert lists example acoustic insulating-glass Rw values **37–43 dB** with configurations such as **8(16)4**, **10(16)4**, and laminated variants. page_0119  
- **Escape-window option:** if this window is intended as an escape opening, Neufert requires clear opening at least **0.9 × 1.2 m** and sill height max. **1.2 m**. page_0109
